# Data provenance

This build uses real-world Indian administrative context: real states, districts, localities, public agencies, registration concepts, utility providers, land-use categories and GIS location context.

The bundled ownership identities, contact credentials, ULPIN-like identifiers, survey numbers, transaction numbers and case numbers are **synthetic application records**. They are intentionally not presented as authoritative government land records.

For authoritative records, import only data for which the operator has legal authorization. In Maharashtra, the official Mahabhulekh/Mahabhumi services provide 7/12, 8A, property-card, mutation and related land-record services.

Never place Aadhaar numbers, personal phone numbers, bank account numbers, OTPs, or other sensitive personal information into the seed database.
