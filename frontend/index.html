❓ Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is ULPIN (Bhu-Aadhaar)?

ULPIN (Unique Land Parcel Identification Number) is a 14-digit alphanumeric code representing the unique geographic identity of every surveyed land parcel across India.

2. How does the 11-step ownership transfer workflow operate?

The transfer progresses from citizen submission, SHA-256 deed validation, ULPIN cadastral check, RoR tenure match, SRO registration deed check, bank mortgage check, court dispute check, to Revenue Officer endorsement and final RoR record update.

3. What is the DPDP Act Privacy Masking?

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, public discovery results mask personal identifying information (such as telephone and full names: R*** K***) while preserving legal title validity verification.
